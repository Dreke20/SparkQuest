Child Safety Standards & Protection Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective immediately for all SparkQuest users globally.

🛡️ Zero Tolerance Policy

SparkQuest maintains an unequivocal, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), grooming, child trafficking, and the presence of any minor under the age of 18 on our platform.

1. Strict 18+ Age Restriction

SparkQuest is exclusively designed, intended, and operated for consenting adults aged 18 and older. Minors under the age of 18 are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, accessing the application, or utilizing any features.

  • Age Verification: Account creation requires confirming that the user is at least 18 years old.
  • Account Termination: Any profile determined or suspected to belong to a minor is immediately and permanently terminated. All associated data is wiped from public discovery and preserved solely for law enforcement reporting where legally required.
  • Hardware & Device Bans: Users who falsify age or attempt to facilitate underage access are permanently hardware-banned across all devices.

2. Prohibited Conduct Regarding Minors

SparkQuest strictly prohibits the following activities without exception:

  • Uploading, storing, sharing, viewing, or transmitting any images, videos, illustrations, audio, or text constituting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
  • Grooming or attempting to communicate with, solicit, entice, or exploit any minor for sexual, commercial, or harmful purposes.
  • Facilitating child sexual trafficking, sextortion, or non-consensual exploitation.
  • Sharing or requesting contact information, social handles, or geolocation data involving minors.

3. Detection, Moderation & Prevention Technologies

To safeguard our community and prevent illicit content from ever propagating:

  • Automated Screening: Images and media uploaded to Supabase Storage are screened against industry-standard perceptual hashes and automated safety filters.
  • Keyword & Behavior Analysis: Chat communications and profile bios are scanned in real-time by automated AI moderation to detect grooming signals, age evasion, or predatory language.
  • Human Trust & Safety Review: Flagged accounts are immediately escalated to human safety moderators for review within 1 hour.

4. In-App Reporting for Users

SparkQuest empowers all users to actively report suspicious activity, suspected underage accounts, or any child safety concern directly within the app:

  • On Profile Cards: Tap the ••• overflow menu on any explorer's card → Select Report / Block → Choose "Child Safety / Underage Concern".
  • In Chat Messages: Tap the 🛡️ Shield or header options in any conversation → Select Report User → Choose "Child Safety / Underage Concern".

Immediate Action: Submitting a Child Safety report immediately blocks the reported user, severs all chat connections, and tags the incident with emergency priority for 24/7 human safety investigation.

5. Mandatory Reporting to Authorities & NCMEC

SparkQuest complies with 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and all applicable federal and international child protection laws. When our safety systems or user reports identify any instance or credible suspicion of CSAM or CSAE:

  • We immediately file a formal report with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline.
  • We coordinate directly with local and federal law enforcement authorities, including the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and international equivalents (Interpol, INHOPE).
  • We preserve all relevant logs, identifiers, and digital evidence strictly for law enforcement transfer pursuant to valid legal requests.
