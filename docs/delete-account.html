Delete Your Account & Data

Under Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, SparkQuest provides this publicly accessible web portal so you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data even if the app has already been uninstalled from your device.

⚠️ Important: Account Deletion is Permanent

Once processed, your SparkQuest explorer profile, verified photos, match history, chat conversations, ember vault balance, quest badges, and season XP will be permanently purged and cannot be recovered.

What Data is Deleted?

  • Explorer Profile: Bio, age, gender identity, orientation, dating preferences, and prompts.
  • Media & Biometric Proofs: All uploaded photos and 3D liveness selfie check frames.
  • Social Graph: Sparks sent/received, active matches, and unread chat messages.
  • Economy: Unspent Embers, inventory items (Roses, Boosts), and VIP tier flags.
  • Location History: All cached coordinates, venue check-in records, and radar beacons.

What Data is Retained (Exceptions)?

In strict compliance with Google Play policy and applicable laws, we may retain:

  • Financial Transaction Records: Google Play order IDs and receipt tokens are retained for legal, financial accounting, and tax compliance purposes. (We never hold credit card numbers).
  • Safety & Abuse Records: If an account was terminated for harassment, threat, or serious safety violations, a secure cryptographic hash of the identifier may be retained for up to 30 days to enforce protective blocks and protect other users.
